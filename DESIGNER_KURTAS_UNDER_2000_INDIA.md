# Designer Kurtas Under ₹2,000 in India

**Last verified: 18 September 2026**

## Scope

This catalogue covers live, direct product listings for standalone kurtas and kurtis priced strictly below ₹2,000 in the India market. Because no gender was specified, it includes **Women, Men, and Kids**. “Designer” is used inclusively for styled, embroidered, embellished, printed, occasion, craft, or branded fashion kurtas/kurtis—not only luxury designer labels. Sets, packs, bottoms, dupattas, scarf bundles, shrug/waistcoat combinations, unavailable products, listings without a verifiable current price, and prices of ₹2,000 or more are excluded.

> **Reality check:** No web list can literally cover the entire internet. This is a broad, evidence-based snapshot of the accessible stores researched; prices, size availability, stock, URLs, and promotions can change after verification.

## Methodology

The two supplied verified research exports were parsed in full through their closing fences: 185 official-store rows and 160 D2C/craft-store rows. Every distinct canonical direct URL was preserved. Supplemental live catalog checks added Janasya (available variants in its product feed), Pantaloons (current category cards paired with direct product URLs), and Flipkart (current search cards and canonical product paths). Displayed whole-rupee prices—not coupon-contingent offer prices—control placement. URLs were stripped of search, affiliate, and tracking parameters. Products were filtered before deduplication; URL is the identity key, so same-named colour/SKU pages remain distinct.

The three ranges are intentionally **non-overlapping**: Under ₹1,000 means ₹0–₹999; ₹1,000–₹1,499 means exactly that range; and ₹1,500–₹1,999 means exactly that range. This prevents duplicate rows even though a lower-priced item would also satisfy a higher spending cap. Within each section, rows are sorted by price ascending, then brand/retailer, then product name.
